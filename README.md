## How to Read & Follow This Repo
* **Always Up to Date (Follow via Diffs):** This repository holds the live working text of Series 2. Pull, browse in GitHub, or inspect commit diffs whenever you want to see new chapters and polish updates as they land.
* **Weekly Saturday Serial ("Rocket Man" Cadence):** Click **Star** and **Watch → Custom → Releases** (or subscribe to the repo's `.atom` releases feed) to get notified each week when the next episode drops.
* **Discussion & Science Q&A:** Have thoughts on the story, Akida spiking neural architecture, basal cognition, or relativistic mechanics? Open a thread in **Discussions** or **Issues**.
